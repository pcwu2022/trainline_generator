# Country boundaries

`country_boundaries.geojson` contains Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 country
geometries, with properties reduced to `iso_a2` (source `ISO_A2_EH`) and `name`
(source `ADMIN`). Downloaded 2026-11-10. Coordinates and geometries are unchanged.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

These generalized boundaries filter cell centers; they are not cadastral or
survey boundaries. Coastline details can differ from the population grid.
